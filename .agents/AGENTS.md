2. **Context Maintenance**: Before finishing a significant task, or when concluding a session, you MUST update `PROJECT_LOG.md` with a brief summary of what was just accomplished, any bugs fixed, and what the next logical steps are.
3. **No Reminders Needed**: You will do this automatically and persistently without the user needing to remind you to "save the context".
